Data & privacy notice

This is an informational summary of what this study tool does with data, written in plain language rather than as a legal document.

What is stored

  • Your star marks, per-word mastery level, attempt/correct counts, and Learn statistics
  • Your interface preferences (theme, text size, study direction, scope, batch size, etc.)

No name, email, or other identifying information is collected by this tool. The word list itself is fixed content built into the app, not user data.

Where it's stored

Data is saved using Claude's built-in artifact storage, scoped privately to your account. It is not shared with other users of this artifact, and this tool does not send data to any third-party server, analytics provider, or advertiser.

How it's used

Stored data is used only to power features you see directly: remembering which words you've starred or mastered, resuming your Learn progress, and remembering your display settings. The Learn algorithm's scheduling runs entirely in your browser using this stored history — no external processing or model is involved.

Your controls

You can permanently delete all stored progress and statistics at any time with the "Reset all progress & stats" button in Customize. Closing this tool without saving generates no data.

This notice describes the tool's actual data handling to help you make an informed choice; it is not a legal certification of compliance with GDPR or any other specific regulation.
