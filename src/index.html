Compliance notice

This is a plain-language explanation of what this tool does with data, not a legal document.

What is stored, and where

Everything this tool remembers is written to your browser's own localStorage for this page — not to a server, not to an account, not to the cloud. Nobody at Anthropic or anywhere else can see it, and it never leaves your device. It includes:

  • Vocabulary customizations — words you've added, and default words you've removed
  • Study progress — star marks, per-word mastery, attempt and correct counts
  • Statistics derived from the above
  • Interface preferences (theme, text size, study direction, scope, batch size, and so on)

No name, email, device identifier, or other personal information is collected. The default word list is fixed content built into the app, not data about you.

What is not collected

This tool has no analytics, tracking, advertising, or telemetry, and makes no network requests of any kind once the page is loaded — everything runs entirely in your browser. There is no consent popup for storage because everything stored is strictly necessary for the features you're actively using (saving your own vocabulary and progress), not optional tracking — there is nothing non-essential to ask consent for.

Persistence and loss of data

Because this is ordinary browser storage, it is tied to this specific browser and device. It will not follow you to another browser or computer, and clearing your browser's site data (or using private/incognito mode) will erase it. If your browser blocks local storage entirely, a notice appears at the top of the page and your changes will only last for the current session.

Your controls

You can add, remove, or restore vocabulary at any time in the Manage tab. You can permanently erase everything this tool has stored — vocabulary changes, progress, statistics, and preferences — with the "Clear all local data" button in Settings.

This notice describes the tool's actual data handling to help you make an informed choice. It is not a certification of compliance with GDPR or any other specific regulation.

About this tool

Free LSAT Vocab Prep is a small, self-contained vocabulary study tool covering words that show up often in dense LSAT reading passages and logical reasoning stems. It runs entirely in your browser — open index.html and go.

Modes

  • Flashcards — flip through terms and definitions at your own pace.
  • Learn — an adaptive quiz that studies small batches at a time, prioritizing whatever you're most likely to forget, and shifting from multiple choice to typed recall as you demonstrate mastery.
  • Match — a timed pairs game.
  • Browse — search the full list, star favorites, or remove words.
  • Manage — add your own words, or restore ones you've removed.
  • Stats — a summary of what you've mastered so far.

Keyboard shortcuts (Flashcards)

  • Space or click the card — flip it
  • Left / Right arrows — previous / next card
  • S — star the current card
  • K — mark the current card known
